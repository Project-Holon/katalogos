# Exp. Share

The whole party gains experience in every battle, as with the Exp. Share of
the later games. The Pokemon that fought get their usual share; every other
party Pokemon that can battle gets half of what it would have gained by
fighting, with its own level, Lucky Egg, trade bonus and effort values.

One option, **Exp. Share**, on by default: switch it off in the settings to
play with the game's own rule again.

It is one rule (`mod/rules/share.rule`) on eidos's `battle.exp` event, and it
needs eidos 0.2.2 or newer (mod interface 5).
