# Blaze Black (Clean, unofficial)

**Unofficial.** This is a conversion of **Blaze Black 3.1 (Clean)**, the ROM hack by **Drayano**, into an eidos
mod. Drayano made the hack; he did not make this conversion and does not support it. Please do not ask him for
help with it.

Drayano's own pages, with the hack, its documentation and his other work:

- [Drayano's thread on Project Pokemon](https://projectpokemon.org/home/forums/topic/13244-pok%C3%A9mon-blaze-black-pok%C3%A9mon-volt-white/)
- [Drayano on X](https://x.com/Drayano60)

## What the mod changes

- The trainers, wild Pokemon, evolutions, items and in-game trades of the Full version.
- The Pokemon themselves, their level-up moves and attacks are left as they are.

The mod's data was converted with kenon from the hack's original patch, so it carries the hack's own changes and
nothing else of the game.

## Options

- **Liberty Pass card** (on): the Liberty Pass, so the ship to Liberty Garden and Victini can be reached.
- **National Pokedex early** (off): the National Pokedex from the start.

The hack's guides did both by editing the save; here they are options in Settings.

## Needs

- eidos 0.2.7 or newer (mod interface 7).
- Pokemon Black: on White the mod shows as Unavailable and is not loaded.
- A new game is best: a save from the plain game keeps its own team and its place in the story.
